# Licensing and attribution

* Statistics Finland municipality layer: the provider's open statistical data is published under CC BY 4.0; retain provider/source attribution and link to the boundary catalogue.
* HSL GTFS: HSL Open Data identifies public transport open data as CC BY 4.0. Attribute Helsinki Region Transport (HSL) and link to its open-data page. The checked-in stop subset is a transformed extract, not a replacement for the source archive.
* Helsinki 3D: the City of Helsinki documents the city model under CC BY 4.0. The application embeds the official viewer and does not copy/rehost model assets; follow source attribution if assets are later ingested.
* OpenStreetMap: tiles/data require visible © OpenStreetMap contributors attribution and ODbL compliance. OSM database-derived products can carry share-alike obligations. Standard tile service is best-effort and its usage policy must be followed.
* Helsinki Service Map: redistribution license for the queried API response has not been verified; no unit data are presently ingested.

This document records provider terms reviewed for the prototype, not legal advice. Recheck terms before adding new source products or redistributing derived databases.
